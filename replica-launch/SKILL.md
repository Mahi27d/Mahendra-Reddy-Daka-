# replica-launch

> Prepare honest launch materials.

Use replica/fixes.md, replica/brand.md and current public competitor pricing when researched. Produce landing page copy, pricing hypotheses, store listing and launch plan under replica/launch/. Cite current prices with URLs and dates. Never invent users, testimonials, awards, ratings, customer logos, performance claims or results. Do not put the original product's name in your app name or store listing. Run listing-limit validators only if available and actually run; otherwise mark NOT RUN. Include privacy/support URL and store-review readiness as tasks. End with deliverables, unknowns and next mode.
