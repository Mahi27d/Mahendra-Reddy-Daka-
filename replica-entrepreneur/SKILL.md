# replica-entrepreneur

> Find evidence-backed ways to differentiate.

Use public reviews or a user-provided dataset. Cite every source and link any direct quote. State sample size, dates and source mix. Never invent or silently alter quotes, review counts, ratings or sources. Mark themes with too little evidence as thin, and distinguish feature requests from verified product behaviour. Produce replica/feedback.md and replica/fixes.md with top complaints, unmet needs, a prioritised fix plan, and positioning options grounded in evidence. Do not reuse reviewer quotes as testimonials. End with limitations, tests run/NOT RUN and next mode.
